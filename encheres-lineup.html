How to play
  • From two to six players (switch in the menu), 35 each. With two players you make 5 picks each, with three or four players 4 picks each, with five or six players 3 picks each (Art: always 5 up to four players).
  • One item is revealed at a time. The player shown next to Opens starts the bidding, then you bid back and forth out loud.
  • Highest bid wins. Tap who won, set the bid, press Sold. The bid comes off their budget.
  • Minimum bid is 1. Max is whatever you have left, so you can go all in.
  • Each player can skip once. A skipped item is gone for good.
  • If everyone else has finished, the last player left gets every next lot for free, even with no credit left.
  • Football and Cinema: every team needs a goalkeeper (football) or a director (cinema). None by your second-to-last pick? A random one is added for free.
  • Each selection is sorted by bid, biggest on top. Made a mistake? Undo any lot from the history.
